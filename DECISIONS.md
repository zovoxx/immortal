# RELAY — Design & Technical Decisions

Decisions made without asking, with the reasoning.

## Engine / tooling
- **Godot 4.7.2 stable** (latest stable 4.x available at build time), official Linux x86_64 binary + export templates.
- **Jolt Physics** is set as the 3D physics engine (`physics/3d/physics_engine`), built into Godot since 4.4.
- No plugins are used.
- Extra autoloads beyond Game/Events/Save: **Settings** (user settings + input rebinding) and **Audio** (procedural placeholder sounds + buses). Keeping these out of `Game` keeps game state serialisation clean.

## Movement
- "Grab ledges up to 2.2 m above you" is measured from the courier's feet at the moment of the grab. Combined with the 1.4 m jump this lets you reach ~3.6 m ledges from the ground, which felt right for chunky placeholder geometry.
- Low obstacles 0.2–1.2 m are vaulted when thin, mantled (stepped onto) when deep. CharacterBody3D doesn't auto-step, so modules use ramps or vaultable steps.
- Jump/crouch/dodge presses are edge-detected once per physics frame inside the courier (not `is_action_just_pressed`) so inputs are never lost between render and physics frames.
- Movement is camera-relative; walking is "hold Walk" on keyboard or a light stick deflection on gamepad.
- Test harness: `tests/run_tests.gd` uses `_initialize()` + one frame wait because autoloads aren't registered while a `--script` main loop is compiled. Engine errors are captured with `OS.add_logger()` (Godot 4.5+ Logger API) for the smoke tests.

## Death, items, corpses
- The **sling is innate** (every courier has one, no slot); sling stones, noise-makers, smoke pots, caltrops and fire pots are the ammo that take slots.
- **Materials (scrap, cloth, oil, feathers) weigh 0 slots** and ride in a pouch, but they *are* dropped on death and can be stolen — otherwise the base 4 slots (Message + kit) would leave no room for the upgrade economy.
- The starting kit may exceed capacity (e.g. Veteran's extra axe); the courier is simply overloaded (slower) until they drop something.
- The Message passes to the next courier and is never left on a corpse (it "cannot be dropped").
- Corpse positions are stored zone-local (zone index + offset from the zone origin) so they survive origin shifts and zone reloads.
- "Continue" always resumes at the last activated relay station with the saved courier.
- Placeholder visual scenes are generated by `tools/build_content.gd`; after generation the `.tscn` files are the source of truth.

## Zones
- Modules sit on a 20 m grid; a module is 1 or 2 cells long and 1 cell (20 m) wide. Exits are straight, left or right. Headings are restricted to +Z/±X so a zone (and the whole road) can never overlap itself; lateral drift is clamped to ±3 cells.
- Each zone ends with a station module heading +Z; the next zone's first module attaches to its exit. Zone k's start cell is the cumulative sum of earlier zones' end cells (computed from data only, cached), so teleporting to zone N is cheap.
- Guarantees are scheduled: a shelter-tagged and a climb-tagged module are placed at seeded positions in every zone; loot caches and encounters are chosen from slot markers (with a fallback position). The climb module is the guaranteed alternate route; several modules also have hidden shortcuts revealed by Old / Fast Courier.
- Edge blocking (cliffs, barriers, ridges, dunes) is generated per biome by ZoneInstance on every cell side that isn't a connection, so module scenes only contain their interior.
- Floating origin: zones are positioned relative to an integer origin cell; when the courier changes zone (or strays > 300 m from the origin) everything is shifted. Corpses/stations are zone-local so nothing drifts.
- Station s lives at the end of zone s-1 (station 0 at the start of zone 0).

## Enemies
- Enemies are built from scripts (`Hound.new()` etc.) with their visuals in `*_model.tscn`; tunables are exported vars set in `_init()` (override them in a scene/inherited script if needed).
- Scavenger theft only happens while the corpse's zone is loaded (no offline simulation). A scavenger alive when its zone unloads takes its loot with it.
- A new scavenger is sent toward the newest corpse ~40 s after a respawn, so going back quickly pays off.
- Enemies ignore a courier inside a relay station's area (stations are safe ground).
