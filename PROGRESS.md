## Log

### Phase 1 — Movement core (done)
- `player/courier.gd`: walk/run/sprint, jump (coyote + buffer), slide, vault/mantle, ledge grab + shimmy + pull-up, wall-run + wall-jump, dodge roll (i-frames), stamina with exhaustion, fall damage (>6 m hurts, >=15 m kills), footstep noise events, scent trail.
- `player/movement_tuning.tres`: every movement number. `player/camera_rig.tscn`: spring-arm orbit, sprint FOV kick, aim mode, shake.
- Input map for KB+M and gamepad (`autoload/input_defaults.gd`, written into project.godot by `tools/setup_input.gd`).
- `world/test_course.tscn`: every obstacle type. Tests: movement suite (speeds, jump height, vault, climb, slide, wall-run, fall damage) + test-course smoke test.

### Phase 2 — Death, respawn and corpses (done)
- Health (no regen), bandage/food use (timed), carry slots (`items/carry_inventory.gd`, weight 1-3, pouch for materials, load penalty 5%/slot past 2), item defs as .tres, pickups, drop, loot caches, loot window UI.
- 10 traits as Resources (`traits/*.tres`), name generator, CourierData. Death: slow-mo + death card, CorpseData record (zone-local pos), corpse body with flag, respawn as a new courier at the last station (`world/death_flow.gd`). Corpse cap 30 (oldest decays).
- Relay station scene (hut + signal fire, activation area, autosave). Roll of the Fallen page.
- `tools/build_content.gd` generates all placeholder visual scenes (`*_model.tscn`).

### Phase 3 — Zone generation (done)
- `world/zone_gen/`: ZoneModule (module root with Entry/Exit sockets, tags, slots), ModuleLibrary (metadata cache), ZoneGenerator (seeded, seed = world_seed + zone), ZoneLayout (pure data), ZoneInstance (builds modules + edge blockers + underlay + runtime navmesh bake), ZoneStreamer (prev/current/next, floating origin).
- 11 Greywood modules + station module generated by `tools/builders/biome_greywood.gd` (trail, long trail, two bends, stream with hidden stepping stones, climb outcrop, ruined hut shelter, rock overhang shelter, tall-grass hollow, gorge bridge with toll slot + hidden trunk, old-tree landmark).
- `world/game_world.tscn`: the Long Road. Starts at the last station, streams zones, rebases origin, compass targets.
- Tests: determinism, 20 connected zones without overlap, per-zone guarantees, navmesh bake, streaming + rebase + station activation.

### Phase 4 — Enemies part 1 (done)
- `enemies/enemy_base.gd`: state machine (IDLE/PATROL/SUSPICIOUS/CHASE/ATTACK/SEARCH/RETURN + FLEE/LOOT/STUNNED/DEAD), vision cone + raycast LOS (smoke blocks it), awareness meter, sound events, watchtower alarms, NavigationAgent3D on the per-zone runtime navmesh (direct steering until baked), Scarred hesitation, debug label.
- `enemies/perception.gd`: crouch / tall grass / night / weather / trait modifiers; scent multipliers (night x1.5, Light-footed x0.5, dust storms disable).
- Hounds: packs, howl alert, scent following point by point (water gaps break the trail), lunge attack, give up when the courier is up high.
- Scavengers: go to the nearest corpse with loot, steal one item every 45 s, flee, fight when cornered, drop a sack with the stolen items.
- `enemies/enemy_spawner.gd`: spawns layout encounters + corpse-drawn scavengers (also after a new death).

### Phase 5 — Enemies part 2 and combat (done)
- `player/courier_combat.gd`: blade light (tap) / charged (hold 0.65 s, staggers), best carried weapon auto-used (or the one chosen with Use), sling aim (RMB/LT, over-the-shoulder camera) + throw (LMB/RT) of the selected throwable or stones.
- `items/projectile.gd` + effects: stones (damage, break lanterns, impact noise), noise-maker (loud rattles enemies investigate), smoke pot (blocks LOS), caltrops (slow + hurt hunters), fire pot (burst + burning ground).
- Dodge roll with i-frames (Phase 1 movement). Toll-keeper: demands a toll that rises with zone, pay (least valuable items first, remembered per zone), sneak while it glances at the bridge, or fight its telegraphed slam. Watchtower: sweeping spotlight, 1 s exposure rings the bell (hunters within 60 m chase), sling stone darkens it 30 s. Rider: gallops on open ground, limited turn rate, predicts the courier's path, crashes (stunned) into trees/walls, pass-by strikes.

### Phase 6 — Weather and day/night (done)
- `world/weather/weather_manager.gd`: seeded storm roll per zone (Difficulty.storm_chance x biome), a storm front that starts ~70 m behind the courier and advances along the zone path (visible curtain `storm_front.tscn`), intensity by distance to the front, rain particles + low visibility, lightning on the highest exposed point near the courier with a 1.5 s flicker + crackle warning, wind gusts that push mid-air, flash floods (WaterVolumes rise) in flood biomes, dust storms (very low visibility, scent disabled). Shelters/stations block lightning and wind.
- `world/environment_controller.gd`: biome palette blending, 12-minute day/night (sun/moon, sky, fog, ambient), storm/dust darkening, lightning flash, Night-eyed vision (clear nights, glare + blur by day).
- Night: enemy sight x0.55, hound scent x1.5 (Perception).

### Phase 7 — Biomes and difficulty (done)
- 5 biomes (`world/biomes/*.tres`) with distinct palettes, edge styles, enemy weights, storm multipliers and hazards; 8-11 modules each + a station module (`tools/builders/biome_*.gd` -> `world/modules/<biome>/`):
  - Greywood Forest (streams with flash floods, hounds), Ruined Highway (cars to vault, overpass climb, tunnel shelter, watchtower slots), Storm Plains (open ground for riders, lone trees/hedges that crash them, windmill lightning rod, 2.6x storms, creek floods), Floating Ruins (platforms over a void with a kill volume, wall-run gap, spire climb, ambient wind gusts), Salt Desert (white glare x1.8, dust storms, extra scavengers, a single shelter type).
- Difficulty (`world/zone_gen/difficulty_curves.tres`): enemy groups, HP, damage, storm chance, toll price rise with zone; loot falls. Long Road +N every 50 zones adds per-loop terms (shown on the HUD).
- Enemies never walk off ledges; anything falling into the void dies.

### Phase 8 — Stations, upgrades, momentum, Message (done)
- `ui/station_menu.gd`: Rest (full heal, +~3 in-game hours, storm may move in, autosave), Stash (6 shared slots, put/take via the loot window), Upgrade bench (Bed per station +20 HP, Forge global Fine Blade, Kennel global one noise-maker per life, Signal Mirror per station forecast). Materials come from caches, toll-keepers, scavengers; spent from pouch first, then stash.
- Momentum: +1 per station reached without dying (max 5): 2 = +5% speed, 3 = better loot, 5 = Fast Courier (hidden shortcuts revealed, par-time material bonus). Death resets it.
- The Message: 8 fragments (`world/message_fragments.gd`), Seal Breaks card every 25 relays, Message page in the menus.
- Tests: save/load full round-trip (zone, lineage, corpses, stash, upgrades, tolls, caches, fragments), upgrades/kennel, momentum, fragments, stash persistence, station menu smoke.
